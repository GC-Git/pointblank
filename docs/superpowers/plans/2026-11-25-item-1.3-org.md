# Item 1.3 plan: soldiers, teams, squads, leaders

## Data (`data/organisation.json`, all placeholder)
The table keeps its loader schema (every value is one sourced scalar), so it is flat:
- `kind: squad` rows: `name`, `side`, `succession_delay` (s), `leader_bonus` (0..1).
- `kind: slot` rows (one per soldier, file order = soldier order): `squad`, `team`, `role`,
  `weapon` (id in `weapons.json`), `rank` (seniority, higher is senior), `succession`
  (0 = the leader; 1, 2, ... = the order of taking over). Teams are the distinct `team` ids in
  order of first appearance.
- Rows: `us_rifle_squad` (12: command, BAR team, two rifle teams), `de_gruppe` (10: Gruppenfuehrer,
  MG team of 3 around the MG, rifle team of 6 under the deputy). Needs two new placeholder weapons
  (BAR, MG).

## Code (`src/Sim/Org`)
- `OrgTables.FromData` parses and validates (one leader, unique succession order, known weapon/role/side).
- `Soldier` (id, role, rank, succession, weapon, position, posture, health, team index),
  `Team`, `Squad` (soldiers, teams, `Leader`, succession timer, `HasEffectiveLeader`,
  `LeaderBonus`). Health changes go through `Squad.SetHealth`, which starts succession at once.
- Succession: when the leader is incapacitated or dead, `Leader` becomes null and a timer of
  `succession_delay` ticks starts. When it expires the eligible (ok or wounded) soldier with the lowest
  `succession` number becomes leader (chosen at expiry, so a candidate who died meanwhile is skipped).
  `LeaderBonus` = data bonus with an effective leader, else 0. Not applied anywhere yet (1.5, 2.2 read it).
- `OrgSystem` (ISimSystem): ticks timers, hashes everything, `SpawnSquad(orgId, x, y)`; inputs
  `SpawnSquadInput` and `SetHealthInput` for scenarios and replays. Ids are sequential.
