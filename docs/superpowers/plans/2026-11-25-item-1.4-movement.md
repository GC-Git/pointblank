# Item 1.4: pathfinding and movement (plan)

Code in `src/Sim/Movement`; data in `data/movement.json` and `data/formation.json` (all placeholder).

## Design

- **Cost model.** Terrain costs are the `move_cost_walk/fast/sneak` columns of `terrain.json`,
  divided by the open-ground cost of the same column, so the mode's speed is "speed on open
  ground" and terrain slows or speeds it relative to that. A step costs length (1, or 1.4142 for a
  diagonal) times the mean of the two tiles' costs, plus the hedge crossing cost (relative the same
  way) when it crosses a hedge edge. One cost drives both A* and the time a walk takes, so the path
  A* calls cheapest is the quickest.
- **A\*.** 8-connected. Heuristic: octile distance times the cheapest terrain cost (admissible and
  consistent). Open list ordered by f, then h, then tile index. No corner cutting: a diagonal is
  refused if any building tile or hedge edge touches the corner. A building is entered or left only
  through a ground-floor door on the wall crossed; inside one building movement is free. Unreachable
  goals return null. Tested for optimality against a brute-force Dijkstra on random maps.
- **Modes (`movement.json`).** `move` 1.5 m/s standing, `move_fast` 3 m/s standing, `sneak` 0.6 m/s
  kneeling (a crouched walk; a crawl would be too slow to be useful, and prone is left for the
  stance orders later). Each names its terrain cost column.
- **Orders.** `MoveOrder(squadId, x, y, mode)`, carried by `MoveOrderInput`. The input calls
  `MovementSystem.Deliver`, which today calls `Execute` at once; item 1.5 puts its delay between the two.
- **Squad movement.** One A* path per squad, from the centre of its able men to the target tile. A
  "guide" walks that path. Men have slots in a staggered column behind the guide along the path
  (5 m between ranks, 4 m sideways; the leader is the guide). Each man picks the nearest free tile to
  his slot (not a building tile, not claimed by an earlier man, not held by an idle man of another
  squad), plans his own A* path to it (replanned at most every 5 ticks when the tile changes) and
  walks it. The guide waits while a man is more than 20 m from his slot. A squad's order ends when the
  guide is at the end and every able man is idle on his goal tile.
- **Deadlock.** Men do not block one another in motion (paths ignore other men), so crossing squads
  cannot lock up; end tiles are exclusive, and the squad with the lower id keeps a contested tile.
  Test: US and German squads swap sides through the 8 m hedge gap, seeds 1-100, jittered starts.
- **State.** Guide and per-man state are hashed in `MovementSystem.AppendState`. Dead and
  incapacitated men are skipped and never move.

## Tests (written first)

`PathFinderTests` (cost, woods detour, hedge gap vs crossing, corner cutting, doors, optimality,
tie determinism), `MovementTests` (arrival and distinct tiles, speed/posture ordering, woods,
hedge gap, casualties, unreachable target, order delivery, hash, crossing seeds 1-100, replay,
performance), plus the 1.3 follow-ups `MapHashReplayTests` and `RestoredSoldierTests`.

## Performance budgets (Debug build; tests allow 3x, best of N)

- 40 squad paths on the 150 x 150 map: 1 s (measured about 0.5 s).
- 40 squads ordered and run for 100 ticks: 2 s (measured about 0.65 s).
