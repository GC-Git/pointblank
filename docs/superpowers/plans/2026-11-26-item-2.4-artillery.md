# Item 2.4 Off-map artillery: plan

**Goal:** call-for-fire with delay and dispersion, HE blast and fragmentation, limited missions; the
`bombard-creep` scenario shows the "bombard and creep" moment.

## Design

- **Data** `data/artillery.json` (new table `artillery`, all placeholder): one entry per side's battery
  (`us_105`, `de_105`): side, missions per battle, call and flight time, guns, rounds per gun, duration,
  aim error and per-round scatter (standard deviations, m), lethal and casualty radius, lethal chance,
  direct-hit radius, posture factors (kneeling, prone), shelter factors (hedge or wall between burst and man,
  building), terrain cover weight, kill and incapacitate shares, suppression at the burst and its radius,
  morale shock and its radius, impact memory (s).
- **Order** `OrderKind.CallFire = 10` at a point. It goes down the chain of command like any order (delay
  applies). At delivery `ArtillerySystem.Request` checks: caller is an HQ or has a radio (`NotObserver`),
  an able man has LOS to the point (`NoLineOfSight`), the side has a battery and missions left
  (`NoMissionsLeft`); off-map points are rejected at issue. Accepted: Done; else Rejected with the note.
  The order touches nothing else (like SetRoe: moves and fire carry on).
- **Mission**: ETA = call time + flight time. Aim point = target + per-mission aim error (one draw per
  axis); each round = aim point + per-round scatter. Rounds are spread evenly over the duration
  (guns x rounds per gun). Normal draws: sum of three uniforms, scaled to unit variance.
- **Per round** (impact at ground level): for every man in the casualty radius, chance = lethal chance
  inside the lethal radius, falling linearly to 0 at the casualty radius; x posture factor; x shelter:
  inside a building (and not in the burst's building) the building factor, else a hedge, wall or ground
  between burst and man (LOS check from the burst) the hedge factor, unless within the direct-hit radius;
  x (1 - cover weight x terrain cover). A casualty is dead, incapacitated or wounded (a second wound
  incapacitates). Then `SuppressionSystem.AddExplosion` (both sides) and `MoraleSystem.AddShock` for each
  squad with an able man in the morale radius (linear falloff to the nearest man). Building damage hook
  (`IStructureDamage`, null until 4.5).
- **SideView**: `FireMissions` (own: status, ETA, rounds left), `MissionsLeft`, `ShellImpacts` (everyone,
  within impact memory), and `IncomingFire` gains explosion events (`Explosion = true`, bearing to the
  burst). The unit AI ducks for explosions but never fires back at a burst.
- **State hash**: missions, missions used per side, impact memory, next id.
- **Scenario** `bombard-creep` / `bombard-creep-control`: German Gruppe prone behind a hedge with its unit
  AI; US platoon HQ on a rise calls a 2-minute mission at tick 0; a US squad is ordered forward at
  `AdvanceTick`. A watch records at `AdvanceTick` defender squad suppression, morale, casualties and whether
  US knowledge covers every surviving defender (truth read inside the runner only).
- **Follow-up**: try `UnitAiSystem` in `pin-open` / `pin-hedges`; keep it only if the 2.1 criteria hold.

## Tests first

1. Data loads; rules read.
2. CallFire: rejected without HQ/radio, without LOS, after missions run out, off map; an HQ with LOS gets a
   mission with ETA = call + flight; impacts begin at ETA, count = guns x rounds, spread over the duration.
3. Dispersion: impacts scatter around the target.
4. Effects: prone behind a hedge far safer than standing in the open; in a building safer; suppression
   from a burst far above a near miss; morale shock.
5. SideView: missions only own; impacts visible to both sides; IncomingFire explosion events.
6. Hash changes with mission state; replay of the scenario matches.
7. Scenario criteria over seeds 1-100; perf check of a 4-gun mission on a busy field.
