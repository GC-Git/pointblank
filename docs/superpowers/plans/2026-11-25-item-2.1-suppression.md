# Item 2.1 Suppression: plan

Goal: incoming fire builds per-soldier suppression that decays; suppression degrades fire,
spotting and movement, and pins squads (fix and assault). Spec §1, §2, §5 ("suppression state
must be visible"), §8.

## Model (`src/Sim/Suppression`)

- `SuppressionRules` from a new table `data/suppression.json` (entry `default`, all placeholder).
- `SuppressionSystem(OrgSystem, SuppressionRules)`: per-soldier level (Fixed, 0..100).
  - Built by `FireSystem` callbacks: `OnNearMiss(event)` adds
    `near_miss × calibre weight × (1 − distance_falloff × d / R)`, R = the fire table's near-miss radius;
    the same amount × `share_factor` reaches squad-mates within `share_radius` of the man.
    `OnHit(soldier)` adds `hit_nearby` to every able soldier of that side within `hit_radius`.
    `AddExplosion(x, y, amount, radius)`: the hook for item 2.4 (linear falloff).
  - Every tick: decay `decay_rate` points/s, × `under_fire_decay_factor` while the man had incoming
    fire within `under_fire_window`. Clamped to 0..100.
  - Soldier state: None / Suppressed (≥ `suppressed_threshold`) / Pinned (≥ `pinned_threshold`,
    released below `pinned_release`). A soldier entering Pinned goes prone.
  - Squad level = mean of able men's levels; squad state from the same thresholds (with the same
    hysteresis on Pinned).
  - Factors (linear in level L/100): dispersion `1 + (dispersion_max − 1)·L`, rate of fire
    `1 − (1 − rate_min)·L` (`rate_pinned` while Pinned), observer `1 − (1 − observer_min)·L`,
    movement speed `1` / `speed_suppressed` / `speed_pinned` (a crawl).
  - All state hashed.
- Wiring (optional ctor params, so older rigs keep working): `FireSystem` (dispersion, cadence,
  callbacks), `SpottingSystem.ObserverFactor`, `MovementSystem` (per-man speed),
  `CommandSystem` (holding). Register `SuppressionSystem` last (after `FireSystem`).

## Orders

New `OrderStatus.Held = 7`: an advance-type move (Move, MoveFast, Sneak) delivered to, or under way
in, a Pinned squad is held (its move stops); when the squad drops out of Pinned it resumes to the
same point. Fall-back, Hold and Fire are never held. Morale (Steady/Shaken/Broken) is
independent: refusal and hesitation still apply at delivery, before holding. Item 2.2 will read
suppression as a morale input; Pinned stays a suppression state, not a morale state.

## Visibility

`SquadView.SuppressionLevel` and `SquadView.Suppression` (state); `SoldierView.Suppression`.
Only for own units; enemies are `ContactView`s with no suppression field.

## Fire follow-ups (1.7 review)

Fix: a Fire order at a non-Tracked contact is area fire at its last-known point, and never ends
`TargetGone` because the unseen man fell. Tests: mask rule, friendly fire behind the target, window
vs solid wall, burst and reload cadence, mixed-ammo Partial/OutOfAmmo, near-miss radius from data,
the fog fix.

## Scenario

`tools/Runner/Scenarios/PinScenario.cs`, ids `pin-open` and `pin-hedges`: a German MG team (MG
crew of a `de_gruppe`, prone) and a US rifle squad 220 m east ordered to `Move` at it; a scripted
fire controller (through `SideView`) keeps the MG firing at the nearest contact. `pin-hedges` adds
three hedge lines between them. Metrics: pinned (0/1), ticks to pin. Test: seeds 1–100, pinned
within 30 s ≥ 80 % open, and ≥ 20 points fewer with hedges.
