# Item 2.3 Unit AI: reactions and drills (plan)

Goal: troops are never idle or stupid. Without orders a squad takes cover, returns fire, faces and
engages contacts; its leader runs simple drills (firing line, fire and movement, specialist tasks).

## Architecture

- `src/Sim/UnitAi/UnitAiBrain.cs`: all decisions. It reads only a `SideView` (own squads, `KnownEnemies`,
  `IncomingFire`, `StatusOf`, `RoeOf`), static public data (map terrain and hedges, movement and fire rules,
  `UnitAiRules`) and its own per-squad memory. It writes only through `IAiEffects` (ids and values). A
  boundary test checks by reflection that no brain field or method parameter is a live sim object or system.
- `src/Sim/UnitAi/UnitAiSystem.cs`: an `ISimSystem` registered after `SpottingSystem` and before `FireSystem`.
  Per tick, for each side (same code), it builds that side's `SideView` and an effects object that rejects
  units of any other side, then runs the brain. Movement runs earlier in the tick, so AI moves start on the
  next tick (100 ms).
- Effectors (internal paths, same as orders): `FireSystem.SetAiFire/StopAiFire` (an AI fire layer per squad,
  with a team mask, below the player's Fire order), `MovementSystem.MoveSoldier` (short local moves of one man
  while his squad has no move), `MovementSystem.SetHeldTeams` (teams that wait during a squad move: bounding
  and rushes), soldier posture.
- `SideView` gains `RoeOf`, `IncomingFire` (fuzzed near misses and hits on own men), and `SoldierView.Moving`.
- ROE: `RoeMode` (FireAtWill, ReturnFireOnly, HoldFire), default FireAtWill, set by `OrderKind.SetRoe`
  (`TargetId` carries the mode) through the chain of command; stored in `CommandSystem` (hashed).

## Order and AI priority

A player order always wins: a Fire order suppresses AI fire for its duration; a move is never replaced by
the AI. Within an order the squad still reacts: AI return fire carries on during a Move (orders no longer
stop AI fire), men take cover posture when stopped, held or pinned, rushes and bounds shape a Move under
fire, and Sneak keeps its posture. MoveFast (and FallBack and routing, which move at MoveFast) is the
explicit exception: no rushes, no bounding, and the men stay up. A routing squad gets no AI.

## Fog-respecting return fire

`IncomingFire` turns each near-miss / hit record into a 16-sector bearing (22.5 degrees) and a distance band
(`distance_band`, rounded) from the victim, never the exact origin. `SourceId` is the firer's id only when he
is already a contact the side knows. Leak trade-off: the side learns that someone fired and roughly from
where (what a soldier hears from the crack and thump); with many victims it could triangulate a little
better, which is what real troops do. Quantization is deterministic, so no RNG is used.

## Behaviour

- Under fire (any own near miss or hit within `under_fire_memory`), after `reaction_time`: stationary men
  take the cover posture of their tile (kneel behind a hedge facing the threat, in woods or a building;
  prone in the open; prone when pinned). If idle, the leader sends men on open tiles to the nearest
  covered tile within `cover_search_radius` (claimed so no two share one).
- Return fire (unless HoldFire or Broken): at the source contact if known, else area fire at the fuzzed point
  (clamped to effective range).
- Contact: an idle squad with a tracked contact kneels; with FireAtWill it fires if within
  `effective_range_factor` x its best weapon range.
- Firing line: when an idle squad with a leader engages, the leader spreads the men abreast across the
  threat bearing (`line_spacing`), sorted by lateral position, each on the best tile next to his slot;
  re-formed if the bearing turns by `line_reform_sectors`.
- Task assignment (`data/unit_ai_tasks.json`): when a contact of the task's `target_kind` is known, the
  team of the task's `role` (BAR, MG) goes to the best covered tile within `search_radius` of a point
  `flank_offset` beyond the end of the line.
- Fire and movement: on a Move/Sneak order in contact, a squad with a leader and 2+ teams splits its teams
  in two groups (alternate team order); one group is held and fires while the other moves; they swap when
  the bounding group is `bound_distance` ahead or has stalled for `stall_time`; all close in near the end.
- Rushes: on a Move under fire, moving men rush for `rush_time` then go down for `down_time`.
- Routing follow-ups: flee picks the nearest reachable of several fallback points; it runs from the mean
  bearing of known enemies within `rout_threat_radius`; the Broken ceiling note is documented.

## Tests first (tests/Sim.Tests/UnitAi)

1. Reaction (seeds 1-100, open and hidden shooter): within 2 s of the first near miss every able man is
   down or the squad fired; 100%.
2. Standing in the open: every tick, every soldier; none under fire (near miss within 3 s) stands on an
   open tile for more than 3 s unless his squad moves at MoveFast. Idle, and moving under fire.
3. Firing line: an idle squad in a column engages; >= 75% of able riflemen fire within 10 s.
4. Fire and movement: during an advance in contact, one group fires while the other moves for a
   measurable fraction of the advance.
5. Task: the BAR team goes to a hedge on the flank facing the threat.
6. Units: bearings, `IncomingFire` hides the origin, ROE order and HoldFire, player Fire priority, return
   fire while moving, flee follow-ups, boundary, hash, perf (40 squads within a budget).
