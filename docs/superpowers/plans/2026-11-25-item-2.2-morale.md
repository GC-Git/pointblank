# Item 2.2 Morale — plan

Goal: replace the 1.5 morale stub with a real, visible morale model that feeds the chain of command.

## Model (`src/Sim/Morale`, numbers in `data/morale.json`, all placeholders)

- `MoraleSystem` keeps per squad a level (Fixed 0..100, start 100, mirrored on `Squad.MoraleLevel`),
  a core state, the tracked leader, able-men count, the starting strength, the last-loss and
  leader-killed ticks and the current reasons. Registered after `SuppressionSystem`.
- Each tick:
  - **Shocks:** every man lost costs `casualty_shock × U(spread_min, spread_max) × (1 + fraction lost)`
    (one RNG draw per loss); the leader going down costs `leader_shock` more. A squad with friendly
    support takes `(1 − support_shock_reduction)` of each shock.
  - **Drain:** `suppression_drain × squad suppression / 100` points per second.
  - **Ceiling:** `100 − fraction lost × casualty_ceiling − leaderless_ceiling (no effective leader)
    − isolated_ceiling (no friendly squad within support_radius) − out_of_contact_ceiling (CommandSystem.InContact false)`.
    Above it the level drifts down at `drift_rate`.
  - **Recovery** (only while the squad is not suppressed and has had no loss for `loss_window`): toward the
    ceiling at `recovery_rate × (1 + LeaderBonus × leader_recovery_weight + support_recovery_bonus? + hq_rally_bonus?)`.
    `LeaderBonus` is applied here.
- **States** (core, with hysteresis): Steady → Shaken below `shaken_below`, back at `shaken_release`;
  any → Broken below `broken_below`; Broken → Routing below `rout_below`; Broken/Routing → Rallying
  at `broken_release` while recovering; Rallying → Steady (or Shaken) at `min(steady_above, ceiling)`,
  back to Broken below `broken_below`.
- **Pinned reconciliation:** there is one notion of pinned, the suppression squad state. The shown
  morale state is `Pinned` when suppression is Pinned and the core state is Steady, Shaken or Rallying;
  Broken and Routing take precedence. `CommandSystem` holds advances on suppression Pinned as before;
  for refusal and delay a Pinned squad counts as Shaken.
- **Routing:** on entering Routing the squad runs (MoveFast) `rout_distance` directly away from the
  nearest enemy its side knows (`SpottingSystem.KnownEnemies`, fog-respecting), or toward its own HQ
  when it knows none; the point is pulled in until it is on the map. Orders other than FallBack are
  refused with note `Routing`, and an order under way is cancelled the same way.
- **Reasons** (`MoraleReason` flags): UnderFire, Pinned, Casualties, HeavyCasualties, LeaderKilled
  (for `leader_killed_memory`), Leaderless, Isolated, OutOfContact, Supported, NearHq; `MoraleReasons.Top`
  orders them by priority.

## Command integration

- `CommandSystem` reads `Squad.Morale`: Shaken/Rallying/Pinned → Shaken rules, Broken/Routing → Broken.
- New `low_morale_delay` (command table): extra seconds × (100 − level)/100, reason `LowMorale`.
- Notes: refusals carry `MoraleShaken`/`MoraleBroken`/`Routing`, hesitation `MoraleShaken`, `Held` carries `Pinned`.
- `SetMoraleInput` stays (test override): with a `MoraleSystem` it forces the level to a value in the band.

## Visible

`SquadView` gains `MoraleLevel`, `MoraleReasons`, `MoraleTopReasons` (own squads only).

## Suppression follow-up

`FireRules.HitsAsNearMisses` (scenario switch, not data) and scenario `pin-open-nocas`. Tune
`data/suppression.json` until no-casualty ≥ 70 %, `pin-open` ≥ 80 %, hedge gap ≥ 20.
Result: `near_miss` 6 → 10, `share_factor` 0.3 → 0.4, `share_radius` 8 → 10, `hit_nearby` 15 → 3, and
`fire.json` `hedge_block_chance` 0.5 → 0.8 (with stronger near misses alone, hedges otherwise pinned ~85 %).
Pin rates: no-casualty 89 %, open 100 %, hedges 55 %.
