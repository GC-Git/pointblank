# Item 1.6: Spotting and fog of war (plan)

Goal: each side knows only the enemies its soldiers have spotted, and that knowledge goes stale.
The UI and the AI read enemies only through `SpottingSystem.KnownEnemies(side)`.

## Design

- **Data:** new table `data/spotting.json` (one entry, `default`, all placeholders): refresh period,
  base rate, near and max range, posture factors, movement factors (still, sneak, move, move fast),
  firing factor and window, `lost_after`, `forget_after`. Loader schema gains `spotting`.
- **Rules (`SpottingRules`):** `Rate(distance, concealment, obscurement, posture, motion, firing,
  observerFactor)` = base × distance factor × (1 − concealment) × (1 − obscurement) × posture ×
  motion × (firing ? firing factor : 1) × observer factor. Distance factor: 1 up to `near_range`,
  `near_range / d` beyond it, 0 past `max_range`. Concealment is the target tile's terrain value.
- **Deterministic accumulation, no RNG.** Each observer–target pair with visible LOS adds
  `rate × elapsed` to its progress; at 1 the target is spotted. Losing LOS (or rate 0) clears the
  pair. Reproducible, no RNG draws to keep in step; spread comes from geometry and terrain.
- **Scheduling:** soldiers are listed in spawn order; on tick `t` the observers with
  `index mod refresh_ticks == t mod refresh_ticks` check every enemy (squared-distance cull past
  `max_range` before any LOS). Every pair is refreshed once per `refresh_ticks`, elapsed =
  `refresh_ticks` ticks.
- **Motion:** the system records, per soldier, the last tick his position changed. He counts as
  moving if that was within the refresh window; his mode comes from `MovementSystem.ModeOf(squad)`
  (new), Move if none.
- **Hooks:** `NoteFired(soldierId, tick)` (item 1.7 calls it; firing within the window raises the
  rate). `ObserverFactor(soldier)` returns 1 today; item 2.1 applies suppression there.
- **Knowledge (`SideKnowledge`):** contacts keyed by enemy soldier id (sorted). A contact holds
  last-seen X/Y, last-seen tick, first-spotted tick, kind (Infantry / MachineGun, from role), seen-by
  soldier id and quality. A refresh updates the contact when the pair has reached 1, or when the
  contact is Tracked and the observer has LOS with a positive rate (the side keeps tracking).
  Quality: Tracked (seen within the last refresh cycle), Stale (out of sight; position frozen),
  Lost (unseen for `lost_after`); removed after `forget_after`. Dead or incapacitated soldiers are
  neither observers nor targets.
- **Query:** `KnownEnemies(side)` returns `ContactView` records (ints, Fixed, enums only; no
  `Soldier` reference). `SpottingDebug` (from `DebugTruth()`) gives live positions, for overlays
  only (item 2.6); its docs say so.
- **Hash:** pairs (sorted by key), last-moved and last-fired ticks, every side's contacts.

## 1.5 follow-ups

1. `CommandSystem` docs: delay reasons and duration are fixed at issue; a later leader death does
   not re-roll.
2. Orders to a squad with no able men are Rejected at issue (`OrderNote.NoAbleMen`).
3. `Issue` returns an `IssueResult`; an unknown unit, a wrong-side unit or an unknown kind is
   rejected (no ticket is touched) and recorded in `Rejections` (hashed). No throw.

## Tests (first)

- Rules come from data; rate falls with distance, concealment, obscurement, prone posture; rises
  with motion (still < sneak < move < move fast) and firing.
- A moving standing enemy is spotted sooner than a still prone one at the same range.
- Property: over a mixed battle, every returned contact was seen: its position equals the target's
  recorded live position at `LastSeenTick`, and its observer had LOS then. Enemies past max range or
  behind a building are never returned.
- A spotted enemy that moves behind a building keeps its last-known position (not live), goes Stale,
  then Lost, then is forgotten.
- `ContactView` exposes no reference to live objects (reflection).
- Hash: knowledge state changes the hash; same seed gives the same hash.
- Perf: 150 vs 150 on the test map, full refresh cycles, mean ms per tick within budget.
- Orders: wrong side / unknown unit rejected without a throw; no able men rejected at issue.
