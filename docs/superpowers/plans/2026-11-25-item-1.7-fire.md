# Item 1.7 — Small-arms fire (plan)

**Goal:** projectile-level small-arms fire: each shot is a 3D line with dispersion from data,
traced through the map and past soldiers to find what it hits. Cover from terrain, hedge block
chance, building walls, ammo and reloads, wounding, near-miss events for item 2.1, and the
`Fire` order through the chain of command.

## Data (all placeholders)

- `weapons.json`: per weapon `dispersion` (mrad, one standard deviation), `max_range` (m),
  `burst_length` (rounds), `burst_pause` (s), `reload_time` (s), `carried_rounds` (rounds,
  magazine included).
- `ammo.json`: `calibre_class` (text: `pistol`, `rifle`, `heavy`).
- New table `fire.json`, entry `default`: `near_miss_radius`, `hedge_block_chance`,
  `body_width`, `body_height_{standing,kneeling,prone}`, `dispersion_{standing,kneeling,prone}`
  (firer posture factors), `moving_factor`, `dispersion_per_100m`, `area_aim_height`,
  `kill_chance`, `incapacitate_chance`.

## Model (`src/Sim/Fire`)

- **Shot line.** Muzzle = firer's ground + posture eye height (floor 0; soldiers have no floor
  yet). Aim point = target's ground + centre of his exposed body (body height by posture, the
  bottom `cover × height` hidden by his tile's terrain cover); for area fire, ground +
  `area_aim_height`. Angular error σ = weapon dispersion × posture factor × (moving_factor if the
  firer moved last tick) × (1 + dispersion_per_100m × d / 100) × suppression (1, item 2.1) ×
  morale (1, item 2.2). Yaw and pitch errors are each σ × an approximate normal (Irwin–Hall,
  sum of 3 uniforms, 3 RNG draws).
- **Trace** (hitscan, `ShotTracer`): a tile walk (exact grid crossings) from the muzzle out to the
  weapon's max range or the map edge. In each tile: ground impact (the line drops below the
  tile's ground). On each edge: a hedge crossed below its top (higher ground + hedge height)
  blocks with `hedge_block_chance` (one RNG draw per crossing); a building's outer wall crossed
  below the roof stops the shot unless it passes through a door/window on that side and floor
  (penetration is item 4.3). A corner crossing is taken as x then y.
- **Soldiers.** Every able soldier but the firer whose closest approach to the line lies within
  the walk is a candidate (friend or foe). At that point: if the line is within `body_width / 2`
  laterally and between his ground and the top of his body: above the cover line it **hits**,
  below it the shot is **stopped by his cover**. Candidates are resolved in order along the
  line, interleaved with the terrain walk, so the first thing reached wins.
- **Near misses.** Every soldier the shot passes (closest approach before the shot stopped)
  within `near_miss_radius`, other than one it hit, gets a `NearMissEvent` (soldier, firer,
  origin, calibre class, distance, tick).
- **Wounding.** Per hit: Dead with `kill_chance`, Incapacitated with `incapacitate_chance`, else
  Wounded (a second wound incapacitates). Applied through `Squad.SetHealth`, so succession reacts.
- **Fire control** (`FireSystem`, registered after `SpottingSystem`). `Order` gains `TargetId`
  (−1 = area fire at X, Y). On delivery the target must be a contact the squad's side knows,
  else Rejected `UnknownTarget`; out of every man's range: Rejected `OutOfRange`. Each able man
  with rounds fires: a fractional shot credit grows at the weapon's rate of fire (random start
  phase), bursts of `burst_length` with `burst_pause`, a reload when the magazine is empty.
  Aim: the live target while the contact is Tracked, else its last-known point. Shooters call
  `SpottingSystem.NoteFired`. Ends (Done) when the target falls or is forgotten (`TargetGone`)
  or every armed able man is empty (`OutOfAmmo`), or on a new order. Fire does not stop a move
  already under way (the moving factor applies).
- **SideView.** `SoldierView.Rounds`, `SquadView.Ammo` (`Ok`, `Partial`, `OutOfAmmo`).
- **For 2.1/2.3 (internal):** `FireSystem.Shots` and `NearMisses` (this tick's events),
  `LastFiredTick` per soldier.
- **State hash:** per-soldier ammo/reload/burst/credit state, squad fire orders and this tick's
  events.

## Tests first (`tests/Sim.Tests/Fire/FireTests.cs`)

1. Rules come from data.
2. Hit rate at 50 m > 150 m > 300 m (many shots over several seeds).
3. Hit rate against a target in woods cover < in the open.
4. Hedge block rate = configured chance ± 0.05.
5. A building wall stops shots.
6. Near-miss events are produced for a soldier beside the target.
7. Fire order at a known contact fires, uses ammo and calls NoteFired; at an unknown contact it
   is rejected; a squad with no ammo stops and `SideView` reports `OutOfAmmo`.
8. Hits wound/kill and succession reacts; determinism (same seed, same hash; new state in hash).
9. Perf: 4 squads a side, area fire for 60 s, within budget.
10. Boundary: the reflection test scans public fields too.
