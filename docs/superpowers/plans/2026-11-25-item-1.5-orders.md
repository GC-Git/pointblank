# Item 1.5: order pipeline with chain of command (plan)

Goal: every order, from the player or the AI, goes through one pipeline that delays it by the
chain of command, may make a shaken unit hesitate or a shaken/broken unit refuse, and exposes its
state for the UI.

## Design

- **Vocabulary** (`src/Sim/Orders/Order.cs`): `OrderKind` = Move, MoveFast, Sneak, FallBack,
  Defend, Ambush, Fire, Smoke, Hold. `Order(Kind, X, Y)` (target in metres; Hold ignores it).
  `OrderKinds.ClassOf`: Move/MoveFast/Sneak = Advance (every move except FallBack counts as an
  advance for now, even a move to the rear; a later item may classify by direction to the enemy),
  FallBack = FallBack, all others = Neutral. FallBack moves at MoveFast. Implemented today: the
  four moves and Hold (stops movement). Defend, Ambush, Fire, Smoke are delivered and finish as
  Done with note `NotImplemented`.
- **Morale stub** (`Org`): `MoraleState` Steady/Shaken/Broken on `Squad`, `SetMoraleInput`.
  No dynamics (item 2.2).
- **HQ** (`data/organisation.json`): squad rows gain `unit_type` (`squad`/`hq`) and `radio`
  (0/1, optional, default squad/0). New placeholder rows `us_platoon_hq` and `de_zugtrupp`
  (3 men each, radio 1). Spawned with `SpawnSquad`.
- **Rules** (`data/command.json`, `CommandRules`): base delay, extra for no leader / out of
  contact / shaken / broken, spread min..max, command radius, hesitate chance and delay, refuse
  chances for shaken and broken. All placeholders.
- **Contact**: an HQ is always in contact. A squad is in contact when an HQ of its side with an
  able man has its centre (of able men) within `command_radius`, or when both the squad and that
  HQ have a radio. No HQ on the map = out of contact.
- **Pipeline** (`CommandSystem`, registered after `OrgSystem`, before `MovementSystem`):
  `IssueOrderInput(side, unitId, order)` → `Issue`: wrong side/unknown unit throws; a move to an
  off-map point is `Rejected` (note `OffMap`) at once; otherwise reasons are computed and the
  delay rolled (`ceil((base + extras) * spread * 10)` ticks, one RNG draw), status `Pending`.
  A new order replaces the unit's ticket (an executing move continues until the new order is
  delivered). Each tick: `Pending`/`Hesitating` count down; at 0 a pending order is *delivered*:
  non-FallBack Advance orders roll refusal (Broken/Shaken chance) → `Refused`; a Shaken unit then
  rolls hesitation (not for FallBack) → `Hesitating` for the hesitate delay; otherwise it
  executes. Moves → `Executing` until movement finishes → `Done`; a failed move → `Rejected` with
  the reason (NoAbleMen, Unreachable, OffMap).
- **Query**: `CommandSystem.StatusOf(unitId)` → `OrderView` (order, status, ticks remaining,
  total delay, `DelayReason` flags NoLeader/Shaken/Broken/OutOfContact, note, issued tick);
  `ReasonsFor(unitId)` previews the reasons for a new order.
- **Movement**: `MoveOrderInput`/`Deliver` removed; `Execute` returns `MoveResult`
  (Started/NoAbleMen/OffMap/Unreachable) and rejects off-map goals instead of clamping; `Stop`.
- **Hash**: tickets, morale, unit type and radio are hashed. Replay file layout is unchanged
  (version stays 2); the crossing test scenario now records `order` entries.

## Tests first (`tests/Sim.Tests/Orders/OrderPipelineTests.cs`)

1. Classification of every kind; FallBack never advance.
2. Pending with reasons and ticks remaining, executes when the count reaches 0.
3. Reasons: leaderless, out of contact (no HQ / far HQ), in contact (near HQ, radio), morale.
4. A new order replaces the pending one.
5. Unimplemented kinds finish as Done/NotImplemented; Hold stops a moving squad.
6. Off-map move rejected (pipeline and `MovementSystem.Execute`).
7. Seeds 1–100: mean delay baseline < leaderless, baseline < out of contact; Broken advance
   refusal ≥ 50 %; Broken fall-back refusal 0; Steady refusal 0; Shaken hesitates sometimes.
8. Hash covers pipeline state; same seed same hash.
